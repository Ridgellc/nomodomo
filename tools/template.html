A RAMEN BAR ON MONTEREY STREET, SAN LUIS OBISPO

Nomō Dōmo loosely translates as “let's get together and celebrate.” So we built a room for exactly that.

ノモドモ

We don't just serve ramen; we serve memories.

Step in from Monterey Street and you're somewhere else: a tranquil, beachside retreat of natural wood and traditional Japanese design. The space is made with hinoki wood, minimal and calm, with a little coastal charm.

The menu is thoughtful and rooted in soulful flavor. Every dish is meant to comfort, inspire, and bring people together.

One spot to slurp, share and linger, right on Monterey Street.
